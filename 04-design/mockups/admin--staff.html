Two roles for now — an Owner can change settings and staff; a Cashier can sell and give up to a set discount. Custom roles, per-permission editing, and the staff audit log arrive in Phase 2.

The table answers the only questions an owner has here: who can sell, how to reach them, what they're allowed to do, whether they can unlock a till, and whether they're still on the team. PIN status is a plain "set / not set" — the value is never shown or recoverable, only re-set. The two guardrails are visible as state, not hidden until an error: the owner's own toggle is locked with a "Last owner" lock, and the action is "Reset PIN" (not "Set") once a PIN exists. A deactivated cashier keeps their history but can't unlock a device. Roles are a fixed two-item choice, and Phase 2's bigger permission story is a promise in plain sight rather than an empty menu.

She'll type this on a paired POS device to unlock it. 4 to 6 digits. You won't be able to view it later, only reset it.

Digits are shown while you type so you can double-check, then hidden for good once saved.
Stored hashed — reaches her device on next sync

Setting a PIN is a focused modal, not an inline field, because it deserves a confirm step and a clear "you can't read this back" contract. Digits stay visible while typing — these are 4–6 digit till PINs, not account passwords, and a mistyped PIN that can't be seen is a support call — but they're never rendered again after save; the stored value is an argon2id hash the device verifies offline. Add-staff reuses the same modal shape with name, optional email, and the two-role choice. Cancel is neutral and sits away from the primary Save. The "reaches her device on next sync" line sets the honest offline-first expectation: a new PIN is usable within about a minute, not instantly on every till.
