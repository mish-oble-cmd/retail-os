How prices include tax

Applies store-wide, to every product and location.
Tax-inclusive — shelf price already contains VAT Tax-exclusive — tax added at the till
Switching modes does not rewrite your shelf prices — a ₱185.00 mango stays ₱185.00 on the tile, but exclusive mode would add VAT on top at the till. Past orders keep the math they were rung up with.

Three cards, three different kinds of caution. Pricing mode is store-wide and scary, so the segmented control spells out both halves in a sentence each and the amber bar answers "what happens to my prices?" before anyone asks. Tax categories show product counts so an edit knows its blast radius, and the exempt-sale footnote heads off the most common conflation (exempt products vs. senior/PWD exempt sales). Tenders encode the ledger rule in the UI: history makes a tender undeletable, and the copy says exactly why. Custom tenders (GCash, bank transfer) are how FR-1.5's configurable list shows up for a Philippine store.
